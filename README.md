# Ferdi-Attacks
## Game Description

A Halloween-themed tower defence game about protecting the food bowl from Ferdi, the sneakiest cat in the neighbourhood. Assemble a team of cats, build towers and flaps, and set clever ambushes across a series of levels. Face off against powerful bosses in turn-based duels and take a break from the battles with playful mini-games. The entire adventure runs from a single HTML file, with no external dependencies.

## Story

It all began in Lisa’s home, where peace and quiet never lasted for long. Leia, Rufio and Esme were forever changing their minds: out onto the balcony, back inside, then off to the kitchen door for another adventure. Lisa was constantly opening doors to let them out—and letting them back in whenever they were ready to return.

Then there was Ferdi, the neighbour’s cat. He liked to linger by the doors, nap on the cat tower as if it were his throne, and watch for an open window. The moment he spotted a chance, he would sneak inside and make a feast of the cats’ food.

With three cats coming and going and Ferdi always on the lookout, Lisa began to feel less like she was managing an ordinary home and more like she was defending a little fortress. Somewhere between opening another door and foiling another food raid, an idea took shape: what if this everyday feline chaos became a game? And so her tower defence adventure was born.
